### Hosszú leírás (Description)

```text
TreeCalc is a calculator that shows the expression tree behind the result.

Type an expression, press equals, and open the tree. The same calculation appears in postfix (Reverse Polish) notation. Play walks the tokens and highlights the matching branch. Tap a token to jump to that step. On iPhone, swipe between the calculator and the tree. On iPad, both stay on screen.

Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and integer powers stay exact fractions. 1 ÷ 3 is 0.(3). 1 ÷ 6 is 0.1(6). 1 ÷ 7 is 0.(142857). 1 ÷ 8 is 0.125. Roots, trigonometry, and logarithms stay rounded, because they are not repeating rationals.

Bases 2, 8, 10, and 16 rewrite the number you are typing, the current result, and every number already in the expression. A radix point works in every base. Decimal 0.5 is hexadecimal 0.8 and binary 0.1.

Sine, cosine, and tangent use degrees or radians. Memory, parentheses, powers, factorial, and bitwise operations are on the keypad. Tst types a nested sample so the tree has something to show.

English and Hungarian follow the language of the device. The theme is automatic, light, or dark. The decimal mark is always a dot.

TreeCalc runs on the device. There is no account, no advertising, and no tracking. The only saved setting is the theme.
```
